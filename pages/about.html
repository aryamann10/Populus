OUR PROJECT

What is Populus?

Populus is an educational web project created to explore population growth and its effects on the environment.

The website explains the relationship between organisms, ecosystems and food webs using simple and interactive web pages.

It also presents possible approaches for reducing environmental pressure and protecting ecosystems.

Our Objective

To present environmental concepts in a simple, interactive and easy-to-understand format.

THE BASICS

What is an Ecosystem?

An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with each other and with non-living components of their environment.

Forests, ponds, grasslands and oceans are examples of ecosystems.

Ecosystem =

Living organisms + Physical environment

POPULATION & ECOSYSTEM

How Population Changes Affect Ecosystems

Population Increase

An increase in a population can increase competition for food, water and habitat.

Population Decrease

A decrease in one species can affect organisms that depend on it.

Ecosystem Balance

Changes in populations can influence relationships between different species.
